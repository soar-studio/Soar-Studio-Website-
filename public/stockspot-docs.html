StockSpot Documentation

StockSpot shows real-time, per-location inventory on your Shopify product pages so shoppers can pick up in store today. They search by city, postal code, address or current location, and the store they choose carries through checkout as a real Shopify pickup option.

New here? Jump to the quickstart, or view the product page for an overview.

Plans

Both plans include a 14-day free trial and are billed through your Shopify invoice. The only difference is how many locations can offer pickup.

  • Starter — $6.99/month: up to 2 pickup locations, full analytics dashboard, native checkout integration.
  • Growth — $14.99/month: up to 5 pickup locations, full analytics dashboard, native checkout integration.

To switch plans, open StockSpot in your Shopify admin and go to Billing.

Install

  1. Open StockSpot on the Shopify App Store and click Install.
  2. Approve the access StockSpot needs: read products, inventory, locations and orders (to show stock and count pickup orders), write delivery customizations (to put the chosen store first at checkout) and write shipping (to turn Shopify local pickup on or off for a location).
  3. Choose Starter or Growth. Your 14-day free trial starts.

Quickstart

The Dashboard has a setup checklist that tracks what's left. The steps are:

1. Choose which locations offer pickup

Go to Locations and switch on Pickup enabled for each store that should offer pickup. StockSpot also turns on Shopify's own local pickup for that location, which Shopify needs before it can offer pickup at checkout.

2. Set your inventory rules

On Inventory Rules, decide how in-store stock is protected from over-selling online. See Inventory rules.

3. Add the widget to your product page

Online Store → Themes → Customize → Product page
→ Add block → Delivery & Store Pickup
→ Save

4. (Optional) Add the pickup summary to your cart page

Add the Pickup Summary block to your cart page's main section to show which store the cart is set to.

Tip: Try the widget on a duplicate theme first, then publish it on your live theme.

Locations

StockSpot lists every location from your Shopify admin. For each one you can set:

  • Pickup enabled and Customer-facing: whether the store offers pickup and appears to shoppers. Your plan sets how many can offer pickup (2 on Starter, 5 on Growth).
  • Display name: the name shoppers see.
  • Hours: opening and closing times plus closed days, shown as "Open now" / "Closed now".
  • Pickup instructions and a pickup ready message, e.g. "Ready in 30 minutes".
  • Directions URL and Address override.
  • Safety stock and Priority.

At the top of the page, Order nearby stores by sets how stores are listed: distance (nearest first), inventory (most in stock first), your priority, or alphabetical. Out-of-stock stores always rank below in-stock ones.

Theme widget

The Delivery & Store Pickup block sits on your product page and gives shoppers two choices: Ship it to me or Pick up in store. When they choose pickup they can:

  • Search by city, postal code or address, or tap Use my current location.
  • See each nearby store with its distance, stock status and open/closed status.
  • See Arriving soon at stores where stock is on the way.
  • Add the item with Add to pick up in-store.

Every label, plus the accent, text, background, border and button colours, can be changed in the theme editor. The widget shows French text on French-language storefronts.

Checkout can only offer one pickup store per order. Once a cart has a pickup item, the widget keeps the shopper on that same store and explains why.

Stock display & search

On Settings:

  • How stock is shown to customers: availability only, exact quantity, low-stock threshold (show the count only when stock is at or below a number, 2 by default), or a custom message such as "Ready today".
  • Search radius: 25 km, 50 km, 100 km or unlimited. Shoppers can still see stores further away with Check all store inventory.
  • Distance units: automatic (miles in the US, kilometres elsewhere), kilometres or miles.
  • Default pickup ready message: used for any store without its own message.

Inventory rules

Choose one way to protect in-store stock:

  • Reduce visible quantity by a buffer: shoppers see Shopify's quantity minus a safety buffer.
  • Turn off pickup below a threshold: pickup is hidden for a store once its stock drops below the number you set.

Then choose which stock counts as available:

  • Shopify pickup availability: follows Shopify, including items inbound to a store by transfer.
  • Physical inventory at pickup location only: only stock already on the shelf.

Pickup rules

Keep products out of pickup completely, e.g. gift cards, preorders or oversized items. Exclude by product, collection, vendor, product type or tag. Values must match exactly as they appear in Shopify admin.

Pickup at checkout

When a shopper adds an item for pickup, the chosen store is saved on the cart. At checkout, StockSpot moves that store's Shopify local pickup option to the top, so it's selected by default. The order is a normal Shopify pickup order, which you prepare and mark as picked up the usual way in Shopify.

Analytics

The Analytics page shows, overall and per store:

  • Pickup widget views
  • Pickup add-to-carts and pickup cart adoption (the share of add-to-carts that chose pickup)
  • Pickup orders and pickup revenue

Troubleshooting

A location isn't showing up for pickup

  1. Check Shopify's own local pickup is on for that location (Settings → Shipping and delivery in Shopify admin).
  2. Check it's marked Pickup enabled on StockSpot's Locations page.
  3. Check it has stock above your safety buffer or threshold on Inventory Rules.

The widget isn't on my product page

App blocks have to be added in the theme editor. Add Delivery & Store Pickup to the product template and save. If your theme's product section doesn't support app blocks, add it to a section that does.

The Pickup Summary doesn't show in my cart drawer

Shopify doesn't support app blocks inside slide-out cart drawers, so the Pickup Summary only works on the full cart page.

Shoppers see stores that are too far away

Set a smaller Search radius on Settings.

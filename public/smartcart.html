Frequently asked

Questions about SmartCart?

Do I need to edit my theme code?

No. SmartCart is a theme app embed. Switch it on under App embeds in the theme editor, and switch it off there to go back to your theme's cart.

How are gifts and shipping made free?

On Growth, SmartCart creates an automatic "SmartCart rewards" discount in your store. At checkout it makes gift items and the cheapest shipping option free once the cart reaches each tier.

Does it work with my discount codes?

Yes, for codes set to combine with product and shipping discounts. The Rewards page lists any of your discounts that don't, so you can update their combinations.

What about other currencies?

Set reward and free shipping amounts in your store's currency. Shoppers using another currency through Shopify Markets see the amounts converted.

Where is the wishlist saved?

In the shopper's own browser. No account is needed, and SmartCart doesn't store shoppers' personal data.

Can I change plans?

Yes. Switch between Starter and Growth anytime with Change plan on SmartCart's home page. Uninstalling SmartCart cancels the subscription. Charges appear on your Shopify invoice.

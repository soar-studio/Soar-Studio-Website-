Frequently asked

Questions about SmartCart?

Will it slow down my store?

No. SmartCart loads async and ships under 15kb. Zero impact on Lighthouse scores or Core Web Vitals.

Does it work with my theme?

Yes — SmartCart ships as an Online Store 2.0 app block and auto-detects your theme styling. Tested on Dawn, Horizon, Impulse, and 100+ custom themes.

Can I A/B test upsells?

Growth and Plus plans include built-in A/B testing. Run two versions of an upsell and SmartCart auto-promotes the winner.

What about checkout extensions?

Plus plan adds Checkout Extensions for post-purchase upsells on Shopify Plus stores. Full one-click upsell flow.

How are AI recommendations trained?

SmartCart analyzes your store's order history to find real pair-together patterns. No generic recs — only what actually sells together in your store.

What if an upsell flops?

SmartCart's dashboard shows every rule's conversion rate and incremental revenue. Pause losers in one click.

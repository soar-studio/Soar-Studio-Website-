How it works

Set up in four steps.

1

Install from the App Store

Pick a plan and start your 14-day trial. StockSpot reads your Shopify locations and inventory.

2

Choose your pickup stores

Turn pickup on per location and add display names, hours and pickup instructions.

3

Add the widget to your theme

Add the "Delivery & Store Pickup" block to your product page. Optionally add "Pickup Summary" to your cart page.

4

Shoppers pick a store

They find a nearby store with stock, add to cart for pickup, and that store is waiting for them at checkout.

Frequently asked

Questions about StockSpot?

Does it work with my Shopify locations?

Yes. StockSpot reads the locations and per-location inventory you already have in Shopify. You choose which of them offer pickup; Starter covers 2 pickup locations and Growth covers 5.

How does pickup reach checkout?

Shoppers choose a store and add the item for pickup. At checkout, that store's Shopify local pickup option is moved to the top, so the order goes to the store they picked.

What if the nearest store is out of stock?

The widget shows other nearby stores that have it, and "Arriving soon" where stock is on the way. If no store has it, the item can still be shipped.

Can I hide exact stock numbers?

Yes. Show "Available" only, exact counts, a count only when stock runs low, or your own message such as "Ready today".

Can I keep some products off pickup?

Yes. Pickup rules exclude products by product, collection, vendor, product type or tag, which is handy for gift cards, preorders or oversized items.

Do I need to edit my theme code?

No. The widget is a theme app block you add in the theme editor. It works on themes that support app blocks on the product page, including Dawn.

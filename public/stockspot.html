Frequently asked

Questions about StockSpot?

How fast are alerts?

StockSpot listens to Shopify inventory webhooks — alerts typically fire within 30 seconds of a stock change.

Does it work with multiple locations?

Yes. Set per-location thresholds or roll up stock across all locations. Growth and Plus plans support multi-location natively.

Will it slow down my store?

No. StockSpot runs entirely in the admin — no theme code or storefront injections. Zero impact on page speed.

Can I filter which products get alerts?

Yes — filter by collection, tag, vendor, product type, or specific SKUs. Only hear about products that matter.

Is there an API?

Plus plan includes a REST API, webhooks, and native Zapier/Make integrations. Full docs on the docs page.

What if I need help?

Email support@soar.studio — real humans, same-day replies during ET business hours.

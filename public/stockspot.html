Frequently asked

Questions about StockSpot?

Does it work with multiple Shopify locations?

Yes — that's the whole point. StockSpot syncs with every location you have in Shopify (POS or otherwise) and shows live inventory per store.

How does click & collect work?

Customer picks a store, checks out normally. The order is tagged for pickup, inventory reserved at that location, and the customer gets an email with pickup instructions and store hours.

Can I offer local delivery too?

Yes. Define delivery zones per store (by postal/zip code or radius). Customers in-zone see a "Local delivery" option alongside pickup.

What happens if the nearest store is out of stock?

StockSpot shows which other stores have it, and offers a "Transfer to my local" option if the merchant has transfers enabled. ETA is calculated from your transfer settings.

Will it slow down my product page?

No. The widget loads async in under 15kb, pulls inventory from a cached edge, and never blocks Shopify's buy button. Zero Lighthouse impact.

Do you integrate with Shopify POS?

Yes — Plus plan. Transfer requests flow into POS as pick lists; completed pickups mark the order fulfilled automatically.

Frequently asked

Common affiliate questions.

How does "lifetime" work exactly?

As long as the merchant you referred stays on a paid SOAR plan, you earn 20% of every payment. Month 1, month 60 — same 20%. If they cancel, your commission for that merchant ends; if they resubscribe, it picks back up.

When and how do I get paid?

Payouts run on the 15th of each month for the previous month's earnings, once your balance is over $100. PayPal, Stripe, and bank transfer (Wise) are all supported.

Is there a minimum audience size?

No. We approve based on fit, not follower count. If your audience includes Shopify merchants, you're a fit — even if it's a newsletter of 500 people.

Can I run paid ads to my affiliate link?

Yes, with two rules: don't bid on SOAR, StockSpot, or SmartCart brand keywords, and don't impersonate us. Everything else is fair game.

What happens if a customer cancels and refunds?

If they refund within the first 30 days, we reverse that commission (standard). After 30 days, your commission stays even if they churn.

Do you support self-referrals?

No — you can't use your own affiliate link on your own Shopify store. But if you're a Shopify agency installing on client stores, those absolutely count.

Is there a second-tier / sub-affiliate program?

Not yet. We're focused on making the core program excellent before layering in sub-affiliates. Likely in 2027.

Can I promote both StockSpot and SmartCart?

Yes. Your link tracks both apps — whichever the merchant installs (or both), you earn on. One application covers the whole SOAR catalog, including apps we launch later.

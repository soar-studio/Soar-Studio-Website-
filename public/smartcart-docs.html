SmartCart Documentation

SmartCart replaces your store's cart with a slide-out drawer: a free shipping or rewards progress bar, add-on suggestions, discount codes, a wishlist and free gifts. This guide covers installing it and every setting.

New here? Follow Install and Turn on the drawer — it takes a few minutes. Or view the product page for an overview.

Plans

Both plans include a 7-day free trial and are billed through your Shopify invoice.

  • Starter — $19.99/month: the cart drawer, free shipping progress bar, add-on suggestions, discount codes, wishlist, urgency notice, low-stock alerts, NEW badges, payment icons and all text and colour settings.
  • Growth — $49.99/month: everything in Starter, plus the tiered rewards bar with up to 3 rewards (free shipping, a gift the shopper chooses, an automatic gift), made free at checkout by the SmartCart rewards discount.

Install

  1. Open SmartCart on the Shopify App Store and click Install.
  2. Approve the access SmartCart needs: read products and inventory (for add-ons, gifts and low-stock alerts), read themes (to check the drawer is switched on) and write discounts (for the rewards discount).
  3. Choose Starter or Growth. Your 7-day free trial starts.

Turn on the drawer

  1. In SmartCart, click Enable SmartCart. The theme editor opens on App embeds.
  2. Switch SmartCart on and click Save.
  3. SmartCart's home page now shows SmartCart is live on your store.
Tip: To try SmartCart before your customers see it, turn it on in a duplicate of your theme and use Preview. To go back to your theme's own cart, switch SmartCart off under App embeds.

All drawer settings below are in the theme editor under App embeds → SmartCart.

Drawer & wishlist

  • Drawer title, empty bag message and empty bag button text.
  • Open cart drawer when a product is added and when the cart icon is clicked.
  • Button color for the checkout and other buttons.
  • Enable wishlist: adds a heart to bag items and a wishlist tab. Shoppers can move items back to the bag. The wishlist is saved in the shopper's browser.

Free shipping bar

  • Free shipping threshold in your store's default currency. Set 0 to hide the bar.
  • Progress message ([amount] is replaced with what's left to spend) and success message.
  • Info tooltip shown on the ⓘ icon, and progress bar color.
Note: The free shipping bar shows progress only. Free shipping itself comes from your store's shipping rates or a free shipping discount — set the threshold to match it. On Growth, a free shipping reward tier can make shipping free instead (see Reward tiers), and the rewards bar replaces this bar.

Urgency & low stock

  • Urgency notice, e.g. Your items aren't reserved, checkout quickly to make sure you don't miss out.
  • Low-stock warning on bag items at or below a stock level you choose, with Last item, order soon and Only [count] left, order soon messages. It works for products that track inventory and stop selling when out of stock.

Add-on suggestions

  • Heading and subheading, e.g. Add a little extra.
  • Add-ons collection (optional): products from it are suggested first, e.g. accessories or low-price items.
  • Recommendation type: related products, or complementary products set up in the Shopify Search & Discovery app.
  • Number of products to show.
  • When an add-on would unlock free shipping, it's listed first with its own subheading.

Discount codes

Shoppers apply and remove discount codes in the drawer. You can change the heading, placeholder, button text and the invalid code message ([code] is replaced with the code entered). Codes are created in your Shopify admin under Discounts.

NEW badge & checkout

  • NEW badge on products with a tag you choose (default new) and, optionally, products added in the last X days.
  • Checkout button text, e.g. Checkout securely.
  • Payment icons for the payment methods enabled in your store.

Reward tiers (Growth)

In SmartCart, open Rewards:

  1. Click Add tier (up to 3). For each tier set the cart total to unlock, the reward and its label in the bar.
  2. Reward types:
    • Free shipping: shipping is free at checkout once the cart reaches the amount.
    • Choose a gift: the shopper picks one gift from up to 10 products you select.
    • Automatic gift: the product is added to the bag automatically.
  3. Rewards stack: shoppers keep the rewards of lower tiers. Turn it off to give only the highest reward reached.
  4. Edit the progress message ([amount], [reward]) and the all rewards unlocked message.
  5. Switch on Show rewards in the cart drawer and click Save.

Gifts appear in the bag at $0.00 with the original price struck through. If the cart drops below a tier, its gift is removed. Gift products must be available on the Online Store sales channel — to keep a gift out of your catalog, hide it from collections and search instead of unpublishing it.

Rewards discount

The drawer adds the gifts, but they're only free once the rewards discount is on. On the Rewards page, click Activate rewards discount. SmartCart creates an automatic discount called SmartCart rewards that makes gift items and the cheapest shipping option free at checkout when the cart reaches their tier. You can pause or delete it in Discounts.

Reward amounts are in your store's currency. Shoppers using another currency through Shopify Markets see them converted.

Discount combinations

If a shopper uses a discount that doesn't combine with the rewards, Shopify applies it instead and gifts and shipping are charged. The Rewards page lists these discounts. To let them work together, open each one and under Combinations tick Product discounts and Shipping discounts (free shipping discounts only have Product discounts).

Troubleshooting

The drawer doesn't open

  1. Check SmartCart is switched on in your live theme: Online Store → Themes → Customize → App embeds, then click Save.
  2. Check Open cart drawer when a product is added and when the cart icon is clicked are on.
  3. If another cart drawer app is installed, turn it off — two drawers conflict.

Gifts or shipping aren't free at checkout

  1. Check the Rewards page says Rewards discount is active.
  2. Check the shopper isn't using a discount listed under discounts that cancel the rewards.
  3. Check the gift product is available on the Online Store sales channel.

No low-stock warning

The product must track inventory and stop selling when out of stock, and its stock must be at or below your warning level.
